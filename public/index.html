Honest answers

Questions, answered straight.

Is this AI, or do real people review my photos?+
It's software we built and trained on published facial-aesthetics research. No, a doctor isn't personally studying your photo, and we won't pretend one is. You're paying for the analysis the model performs against the research, instantly. Every recommendation is cited so you can verify it.
Will you give me a rating out of ten?+
No. We think rating a person's face out of ten is exactly what makes these apps harmful. We assess specific, changeable areas, skin, grooming, hairstyle fit, definition, and tell you where there's room to grow. Never an overall verdict on you.
Will you ever recommend surgery?+
Never. Every recommendation is something you can do at home, on a budget. Softmaxxing only, grooming, skin, hair, fitness, style. If you want surgical advice, that's a conversation for a licensed professional, not an app.
Is it really one-time?+
Yes. Charged once, plan is yours to keep. Optional extras exist if you want ongoing progress tracking, but nothing recurring is ever required or auto-enabled.
Does this work for women too?+
Yes. The analysis covers skin, hair, facial structure, and grooming for both men and women. The research the model is trained on covers both sexes. Recommendations are tailored to your specific features, not a generic template.
